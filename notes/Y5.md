# Y5 notes (Youssef)
- Arabic records have no spaCy entities, so their entity features are 1.0. With these features the twin head predicts "contradicted" for 97% of `_A` twins (supported recall 1.8%), which inflates its Arabic ESR. twin_noent removes this and is the fair Arabic system (macro-F1 AR 0.597 vs EN 0.605).
- Use paired ESR (`results/Y5/diagnostics.csv`) next to ESR in the report: it only counts sources whose pre-swap verdict was not already contradicted.
- Evidence sensitivity is the real language gap: paired ESR 40.5 (AR) vs 58.5 (EN) for twin_noent.
