# F3: Authenticity detector, image leakage test, baselines

Notebook: `notebooks/Feba_F3_authenticity_leakage.ipynb` (Colab T4, run top to bottom after F1 and F2).

## Same input for every image

AI images in GenImage are fixed-size PNGs and real photos are JPEGs of many sizes, so a detector can tell them apart without looking at the content. Before CLIP sees an image, every image goes through the same steps:

1. Open the original file and keep only the pixels (format, file name and metadata are dropped).
2. Crop the centre square and resize to 96×96. BigGAN images are 128 px, so every image is resized at least once.
3. Save as JPEG with the compression settings of a real training photo (500 photos sampled, one picked per image from its MD5). Real and AI images get the same mix of settings.
4. Match the share of greyscale images. Some real photos and some AI images are greyscale; in each split the class with the smaller share gets extra greyscale images until the shares are equal (train 2.15% real, 2.14% AI).

34 real photos are smaller than 96 px and are enlarged (train 22, validation 3, test 9).

## Detector

CLIP ViT-L/14 image embeddings (768-d, FP16) of the 96 px images, then logistic regression and linear SVM (C = 1, seed 42). Trained on real + BigGAN, tested on BigGAN (validation) and on Stable Diffusion v1.4 (test, never seen in training).

| Input | Model | BigGAN accuracy | BigGAN AUROC | SD v1.4 accuracy | SD v1.4 AUROC | SD v1.4 recall |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| File format and size only | LR | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Greyscale + JPEG blockiness of the 96 px input | LR | 0.546 | 0.607 | 0.520 | 0.486 | 0.773 |
| F2 embeddings (256 px padded copies) | LR | 0.995 | 1.000 | 0.496 | 0.536 | 0.002 |
| F2 embeddings (256 px padded copies) | SVM | 0.999 | 1.000 | 0.498 | 0.528 | 0.001 |
| **Detector (96 px, same steps for all)** | LR | 0.958 | 0.992 | 0.542 | 0.620 | 0.156 |
| **Detector (96 px, same steps for all)** | SVM | 0.981 | 0.996 | 0.556 | 0.666 | 0.152 |

- File format and size alone separate real from AI perfectly, so the shortcut is real. The detector never receives this information.
- The simple cues left in the 96 px input are close to chance on SD v1.4 (AUROC 0.486) and weak on BigGAN (0.607).
- On SD v1.4 the detector ranks AI images higher than the F2 embeddings did (AUROC 0.620 / 0.666 vs 0.536 / 0.528) and finds more of them (recall 0.156 / 0.152 vs 0.002 / 0.001). It still misses most of them.

## Leakage test on VERITE

218 VERITE photos with a true and a miscaptioned caption and a VAE twin (train 150, validation 32, test 36). Three logistic regression models, trained on original training photos only:

- decoupled: SigLIP 2 image–caption similarity;
- fused: similarity, the detector's authenticity score and their product;
- fused + image embedding: fused plus 16 PCA components of the CLIP image embedding.

A pair is right when the true caption gets a higher probability than the miscaptioned one. ALR is the share of pairs whose verdict changes between original and twin.

| Split | Model | Original acc. | Twin acc. | Changed | ALR |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Validation (32) | decoupled | 81.3% | 71.9% | 3 | 9.4% |
| Validation (32) | fused | 78.1% | 68.8% | 5 | 15.6% |
| Validation (32) | fused + image embedding | 78.1% | 68.8% | 3 | 9.4% |
| Test (36) | decoupled | 52.8% | 52.8% | 4 | 11.1% |
| Test (36) | fused | 55.6% | 55.6% | 8 | 22.2% |
| Test (36) | fused + image embedding | 55.6% | 52.8% | 9 | 25.0% |

On test, adding the authenticity score doubles the changed verdicts (4 to 8) without a clear gain in accuracy.

## Limitations

- Only one unseen generator (SD v1.4) is tested.
- Real and AI images can still differ in content and style; the preprocessing cannot remove that.
- At 96 px the detector sees less detail.
- VERITE is small: one changed verdict moves test ALR by 2.8 points.
