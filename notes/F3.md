# F3: Detector, leakage test, baselines, MAAI901 skeleton

- **Probe:** a CLIP ViT-L/14 logistic probe on GenImage (5,000 train / 1,000 val per class). The Midjourney test images are not on Drive, so I used leave-one-generator-out. AUROC: seen 0.999, unseen BigGAN 0.981, unseen SD 0.870 (`results/F3/probe_metrics.csv`). There is no "edited" class: no edited images were available.
- **Format check:** in our GenImage subset every fake is PNG and every real is JPEG. The seen probe on 900 validation images re-saved as JPEG 85 gives AUROC 0.999. The unchanged images reproduce the stored F2 embeddings (cosine 1.000).
- **Leakage (VERITE):** I compared decoupled, fused and fused-without-`p_ai` models. ALR and its 95% CI are in `results/F3/alr.csv`. `resave_flip` is how often the verdict changes between the raw photo and its JPEG-85 re-save.
- **Baselines on English originals:** claim-only (TF-IDF + logistic regression) and similarity (12 lexical features + LightGBM, 3 seeds) on the MultiFC and AVeriTeC test sets (`results/F3/baselines_originals.csv`).
- **Skeleton:** `reports/maai901_report/main.tex` has 12 sections. Vision references still need to be added to `references.bib`. W&B runs are offline and need `wandb sync`.
