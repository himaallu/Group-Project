# F5: Vision experiments

- **Detectors:** ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16 (`src/vision/train_detector.py`), last 4 blocks + head trainable, 3 epochs, with crop, flip, JPEG 60–95 and blur augmentation. Trained on the GenImage train folder and tested on the validation folder in three settings: seen (real + BigGAN + SD), unseen BigGAN (train real + SD) and unseen SD (train real + BigGAN, the project split). Seed 42; seen repeated with seeds 43 and 44. Run on Colab (L4), logged to W&B.
- **Results** (`results/F5/cnn_vit.csv`, clean accuracy / AUROC): seen ResNet-50 0.974 / 0.996, ViT-B/16 0.984 / 0.998 (mean of 3 seeds), CLIP probe 0.988 / 0.999; unseen BigGAN 0.725 / 0.890, 0.785 / 0.902, 0.860 / 0.981; unseen SD 0.501 / 0.708, 0.534 / 0.881, 0.507 / 0.870. On unseen SD almost no SD image is flagged at the 0.5 threshold.
- **Robustness:** JPEG 50, JPEG 70 and 20% crop for every model and setting. In the seen setting the detectors change by less than 1 point; the CLIP probe drops to 0.820 under JPEG 50.
- **Grad-CAM and ONNX:** `reports/figures/fig_gradcam_resnet50.png` (8 images) with P(AI) per image in `results/F5/gradcam_*.csv`; ONNX ResNet-50 27.96 ms and ViT-B/16 92.63 ms per image on CPU (`results/F5/onnx_latency.csv`).
- **Open items:** the ViT-B/16 Grad-CAM figure needs a rerun of `src.vision.gradcam`; unseen settings use seed 42 only; the unseen-SD images are not the F1 manifest split, so numbers are not directly comparable with F3.
