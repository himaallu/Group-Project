# F2: Vision features and image twins

- **Embeddings:** SigLIP 2 + CLIP ViT-L/14 for VERITE (663 images, 1,001 captions), M4FC (603 images, 770 Arabic claims) and GenImage (18,000 images). There are no GenImage captions, and the Midjourney test images are missing from Drive.
- **Twins:** VAE round-trip gave 452 kept twin images (494 made). SDXL img2img at strength 0.15 gave 148 kept (249 sources). Twins were kept if SigLIP cosine was at least 0.92. Large faces were removed (1 VAE, 9 SDXL), and 30 SDXL pairs were checked by eye.
- **Shared format:** `src/vision/load_verite.py` builds `verite.jsonl` (true → supported; miscaptioned and out-of-context → contradicted; F1 image split 701/149/151). `src/vision/make_twin_records.py` makes one `_V` record per claim on each twin photo (903 VAE, 295 SDXL). All pass `src.eval.loaders --check`.
- **Privacy:** no images are in git. Paths are repo-relative and point to Feba's Drive layout.
- **Limitations:** the Haar face detector misses side-on faces. Only 30 SDXL pairs were checked by eye. The unseen-generator test needs Midjourney or another held-out generator (see F3/F5).
