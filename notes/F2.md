# F2: Vision features and image twins

Notebook: `notebooks/Feba_F2_vision_features.ipynb` (Colab, run top to bottom after F1).

## Embeddings

Models: SigLIP 2 (`google/siglip2-so400m-patch14-384`, 1152-d) and CLIP ViT-L/14 (`openai/clip-vit-large-patch14`, 768-d). FP16 inference, stored as float32, raw and L2-normalised.

| Data | SigLIP 2 | CLIP | Input |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GenImage (F1 split) | 13,000 | 13,000 | 256×256 JPEG copies |
| VERITE images | 664 | 664 | original images |
| VERITE captions | 1,001 | – | caption text |
| M4FC images | 603 | 603 | clean copies of Y1 images |

## Image twins

| Source | Method | Candidates | Kept | Rule |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| VERITE true images | VAE round trip (`stabilityai/sd-vae-ft-mse`) | 338 | 218 | manual face exclusion (39), SigLIP 2 cosine ≥ 0.92 |
| M4FC true-caption images | VAE round trip | 155 | 21 | manual face exclusion (11), CLIP and SigLIP 2 cosine ≥ 0.92 |
| GenImage test (250 real, 250 SD) | SDXL img2img (strength 0.25, 20 steps) | 500 | 440 | SigLIP 2 cosine ≥ 0.70, JPEG 85 |

- VERITE: VAE twins were made for all 664 images; 473 pass 0.92. SSIM was computed (mean 0.77) but not used to exclude. 30 pairs were put on contact sheets for a visual check.
- M4FC: 107 of 144 pass on CLIP but only 22 on SigLIP 2, so 21 pass both.
- SDXL: mean SigLIP 2 cosine 0.83 (real 0.76, SD 0.89). At 0.92, 111 pairs would pass (8 real).

## Shared records

Built with `python -m src.vision.make_twin_records`. Each record keeps the claim, label and split of its source.

- `data/copies/vision/vae.jsonl`: 436 `_V` records, one for each caption of the 218 VERITE twins.
- `data/copies/vision/m4fc_vae.jsonl`: 42 `_V` records, the Arabic true caption (supported) and the false claim (contradicted) of each of the 21 M4FC twins. Sources: `data/processed/ar/m4fc_ar_test.jsonl`, all test.

## Files

- `features/vision/`: row manifests for every embedding array (`genimage_embedding_manifest.csv`, `verite_image_embedding_manifest.csv`, `verite_caption_embedding_manifest.csv`, `verite_twin_embedding_manifest.csv`, `m4fc_image_embedding_manifest.csv`).
- `data/copies/vision/`: `verite_vae_twins.csv`, `m4fc_vae_twins.csv`, `sdxl_twins.csv`, their similarity tables, `sdxl_generation_manifest.csv`, `manifests/vae_manifest.csv`, `vae.jsonl`, `m4fc_vae.jsonl`.
- `data/processed/vision/m4fc_f2_clean_manifest.csv`: the 603 M4FC images (train 363, dev 70, test 170).
- `results/F2/summaries/`: one JSON summary per step.
- The `.npy` arrays and all images are on Drive under `MyDrive/TwinCheck/` (same paths as the manifests), not in git.

## Limitations

- GenImage embeddings come from the 256×256 copies, where BigGAN images have a grey border (F1).
- The SDXL threshold (0.70) is lower than the 0.92 used for VAE twins, and half of the SDXL sources are AI images.
- Few M4FC twins pass (21 of 144).
- Face screening is manual review of OpenCV-flagged images; small or turned faces can be missed.
