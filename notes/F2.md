# F2 — Vision Features and Image Twins

- **Embeddings:** SigLIP 2 and CLIP ViT-L/14 image embeddings for VERITE (663), M4FC (603), and GenImage (18,000). SigLIP 2 text embeddings: VERITE captions (1,001) and M4FC Arabic claims (770). 
- **GenImage splits:** the embedding manifest's `split` column is the GenImage source folder. Experiment splits come from F1: train/validation real + BigGAN, unseen-generator test real + Stable Diffusion v1.4. All 13,000 split images have embeddings (join on `image_id` = `md5`).
- **Twins:** VAE round-trip: 452 retained twin images (494 generated). SDXL img2img at strength 0.15, seed 42: 148 retained (249 sources). Similarity threshold: SigLIP cosine ≥ 0.92. Face screening removed one additional VAE twin; 9 SDXL pairs were excluded for faces. 30 SDXL pairs were visually inspected.
- **JPEG85:** 600 final original/twin pairs (1,200 files): 311 VERITE VAE, 141 M4FC VAE, 148 SDXL. Final SigLIP 2 shape `(1200, 1152)`; CLIP shape `(1200, 768)`.
- **Shared records:** `vae.jsonl` has 903 records; `sdxl.jsonl` has 295 records; `sources.csv` has 249 approved sources. Twin records preserve source claim/label/evidence/language/split and add twin image path and method/similarity.
- **VERITE:** `true` → supported; `miscaptioned`/`out-of-context` → contradicted. Image-group split seed 42: 701/149/151 records (train/val/test). No image leakage between splits.
- **Validation:** checked embeddings passed the recorded audit; final JPEG85 embeddings are finite and normalized; twin JSONL records pass shared-loader validation.
- **Limitations:** M4FC text embeddings are Arabic claims (not captions); only 30 SDXL pairs were manually reviewed; Haar frontal-face screening is limited.
- **Privacy/licensing:** images are not committed to Git. GenImage mirror declares CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0; license terms apply.
- **Status:** Core F2 pipeline completed and validated; review limitations are documented. See `results/F2/README.md` and `features/vision/F2_completion_report.md` for the detailed record.
