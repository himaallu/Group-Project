# H5 notes (Himasri, Wed 18 Nov, early) — zero-shot NLI run

- The reranker is the most important component: without relevance weighting, macro-F1 drops 4–6 points, ESR ~20 and unrelated ~19 points (p < 0.001 every seed). This corrects my H3 guess that saturated relevance scores would make it matter little: they barely rank a claim's own snippets, but they separate related from unrelated evidence, which is exactly what `_C` and much of `_B` need.
- Entity match helps less: −1.4 F1 and −3 ESR points on average, significant only for seed 42; with zero-shot NLI the swap is mostly caught through relevance and the twin loss.
- AVeriTeC (429 test claims, 33 unverified): twin ≈ notwin (38.3 vs 38.0 macro-F1, n.s.), so training on MultiFC twins does not hurt transfer; seed variance is large (sd 2–4.5) because the set is small. Ablated models have lower F1 but higher AUROC there; read with care.
- Calibration fitted on MultiFC val roughly halves ECE on AVeriTeC for notwin (9.8 → 5.0) but helps twin less (11.0 → 9.0).
