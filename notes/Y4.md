# Y4 notes (Youssef)
- Translating 3,670 texts with Qwen3-8B 4-bit on a T4 took 158 minutes; the cache makes the run resumable after a Colab disconnect.
- 20% of twins were dropped (164 failed texts, 4.5%); `_A` and `_B_A` lose most because they need every snippet translated.
- The automatic check (script share) misses short errors: one English word or a few Hebrew letters inside an Arabic sentence, and meaning changes (negation added, country swapped). See `results/Y6/arabic_twin_failures.csv`.
- The glossary is applied as a hint, not forced: Qwen sometimes still spells a name differently (Bush as بوس). Forcing glossary spellings after translation would fix this.
- Hand check of 50 translations (LLM pre-check, reviewed by Youssef): 27/50 (54%) keep every fact that matters for the verdict; 13/25 claims, 14/25 snippets. Name spelling and key terms are the main failures.
