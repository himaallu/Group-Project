# F6: Vision error analysis, T1 ratings, MAAI901 sections

Notebook: `notebooks/F6_error_cases_colab.ipynb` (Colab T4, run after F5). Results in `results/F6/`.

## Setup

The F5 detectors (ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16, seed 42, threshold 0.5) score every validation and test image, using the same 96 px input as F3 and F5. The scores reproduce `results/F5/cnn_vit.csv` exactly.

## Errors

| Split | Group | ResNet-50 | ViT-B/16 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Validation | BigGAN missed | 313 of 1,000 | 9 of 1,000 |
| Validation | Real flagged as AI | 2 of 1,000 | 8 of 1,000 |
| Test | Stable Diffusion missed | 987 of 1,000 | 874 of 1,000 |
| Test | Real flagged as AI | 4 of 1,000 | 16 of 1,000 |

- Stable Diffusion: 872 missed by both models; ViT-B/16 alone catches 115, ResNet-50 alone 2.
- ResNet-50's misses are not limited to the new generator: it also misses 31% of BigGAN.

## 8 cases with Grad-CAM

Fixed rules on the test split (`results/F6/cases.csv`, `reports/figures/fig_f6_error_cases.png`):

| Cases | Type | ResNet-50 P(AI) | ViT-B/16 P(AI) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Stable Diffusion, confident miss by both | 0.0000, 0.0000 | 0.0002, 0.0003 |
| 3–4 | Stable Diffusion, borderline miss by both | 0.078, 0.001 | 0.491, 0.483 |
| 5–6 | Stable Diffusion, caught by ViT-B/16 only | 0.069, 0.034 | 0.9996, 0.9995 |
| 7–8 | Real photo flagged as AI | 0.013, 0.007 | 0.998, 0.995 |

- ResNet-50's maps are small spots, often in a corner or on the background; in case 8 the map is almost flat.
- ViT-B/16's maps spread over the whole image, background included, in every case.
- The scores above 0.99 all come from ViT-B/16: two correct catches (cases 5–6) and two real photos flagged as AI (cases 7–8).

## Image size

The detectors never see the original files, but real photos can still differ in how much detail survives the resize to 96 px. ResNet-50 flags 2 of the 10 validation photos whose shorter side is under 128 px, and none of the 990 larger ones. With so few small photos this is an observation only. ViT-B/16 shows no such pattern.

## Data audit

In the original files, AI images are PNG with one size per generator (BigGAN 128×128, Stable Diffusion 512×512) and real images are JPEG of many sizes, mostly not square (`results/F6/data_audit.csv`). F3 shows this cue alone separates the classes perfectly; F3–F6 remove it by giving every image the same input.

## T1

Feba rated the 40 English notes (`results/T1/sheet_feba.csv`). Agreement with Himasri, Cohen's kappa (`results/T1/human_eval_summary.json`):

- verdict correct 0.494;
- sentences supported 0.349;

## Limitations

- One seed and one unseen generator.
- The 8 cases illustrate failure types; they do not estimate error rates.
- Grad-CAM shows where the evidence for a decision lies, not why the model uses it.
- The case images are not in git (GenImage licence).
