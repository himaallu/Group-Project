# F6: Vision error analysis, T1 ratings, MAAI901 sections

- **Setup:** the F5 detectors (ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16, seed 42, threshold 0.5) scored every validation and test image. The scores reproduce `results/F5/cnn_vit.csv` exactly. Colab (L4), `notebooks/F6_error_cases_colab.ipynb`. Results are in `results/F6/`.
- **Errors (test):** ResNet-50 misses 988 of the 1,000 Stable Diffusion images and ViT-B/16 misses 920. Both models miss 919 of them; ViT-B/16 alone catches 69. ResNet-50 wrongly flags 2 of 1,000 real images and ViT-B/16 flags 4.
- **8 cases (fixed rules):** 2 confident misses, 2 borderline misses, 2 caught by ViT-B/16 only, and 2 real images flagged as AI (`results/F6/cases.csv`, `reports/figures/fig_f6_error_cases.png`).
- **Grad-CAM:**
  - In the confident and borderline misses, ResNet-50's map lies on the background or the corners, not on the animal.
  - ViT-B/16 gives cases 3–4 scores just below the threshold (0.497 and 0.491) and cases 5–6 scores of 0.999, with maps spread over the background.
  - The two false positives are model-specific. Case 7 (560×457) is flagged only by ViT-B/16 (0.977). Case 8 (151×100) is flagged only by ResNet-50 (0.930), with its map on the animal.
- **Image size:** ResNet-50's two false positives are both small (shorter sides 100 and 115 px), while only 5.7% of real test images have a shorter side of 224 px or less. With 2 images this is an observation only.
- **Data audit:** in every split the AI images are PNG with one size per generator (BigGAN 128×128, Stable Diffusion 512×512), and all real images are JPEG of varying size. Grommelt et al. (ECCV 2024 Workshops) report that GenImage detectors learn such cues. Whether ours rely on size or resampling has not been tested.
- **T1:** Feba rated the 40 English notes (`results/T1/sheet_feba.csv`). Agreement with Himasri, Cohen's kappa (`results/T1/human_eval_summary.json`):
  - verdict correct 0.494;
  - sentences supported 0.349;
  - readability 0.477 (quadratic-weighted).
- **MAAI901 report:** Introduction, Related Work, Data, Methods, Experimental Setup and the four Results sections are written, followed by a 12-section full draft. 20 references were checked online. The draft is kept for Overleaf and is not in the repo.
- **Limitations:** one seed and one unseen generator. The 8 cases illustrate failure types; they do not estimate error rates. The case images are not in git (GenImage licence).
