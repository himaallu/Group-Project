# R1 · English data (Rathi, Tue 17 Nov 2026)

- The Hugging Face MultiFC mirror (`pszemraj/multi_fc`) and the Codalab zip have **no snippet text**; we use the authors' `multi_fc_publicdata.zip` (Hansen et al. 2021 copy, Dropbox link in `src/data/download_en.py`), which has the snippets for 34,836 claims. Licence unknown: research use only, cite Augenstein et al. (2019).
- Label map (`data/label_map.csv`): all 116 MultiFC labels in train+dev listed by hand. Of 31,433 MultiFC claims, 9,877 were dropped by the map (half true, misleading, satire, PolitiFact promise/flip ratings, and 2,648 GossipCop 0–10 scores whose meaning is not documented); 233 AVeriTeC "Conflicting Evidence/Cherrypicking" claims dropped. Youssef's Arabic rows are included.
- Cleaning, MultiFC (197,258 snippets in → 154,277 out): rule 1 fact-check sites/pages −25,993 (politifact.com 13,585, snopes.com 4,002, factcheck.org 1,847); rule 2 claim repeats −16,769; rule 3 −219 repeated snippets and −73 duplicate claims (CLAUDE.md's 202 counts all labels, incl. dropped/test); rule 4: 426 claims left with no snippets → `multifc_nosnippets.jsonl`. AVeriTeC (8,546 → 7,671): rule 1 −483, rule 2 −331, rule 3 −61 snippets and −122 duplicate claims (test copy kept), rule 4: 222 claims.
- Final counts (supported / contradicted / unverified): MultiFC train 5,691/10,561/595, val 713/1,317/74, test 705/1,325/76; AVeriTeC train 636/1,449/224, val 71/156/25, test 106/290/33. No claim appears in two splits. Unverified is small (~3.5% MultiFC, ~8% AVeriTeC): use macro-F1 and expect wide CIs for that class.
- AVeriTeC snippets are built as "question? answer" (boolean answers add their explanation) because answers like "Yes" mean nothing alone; web.archive.org links are unwrapped so `domain` is the original site. scikit-learn/pyarrow are blocked on the R1 laptop, so the split is plain Python (`common.stratified_split`).
