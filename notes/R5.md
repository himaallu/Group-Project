# R5 · Explanations + offline index (Rathi) — summary

1. Offline evidence library: 163,244 unique snippets (MultiFC, AVeriTeC, X-FACT AR); BM25 + BGE-M3 FAISS, top 20 each, reranked by our fine-tuned reranker; hybrid recall@5 = 0.945 EN / 0.92 AR, better than BM25 (0.91 / 0.86) or dense (0.87 / 0.80) alone.
2. Cited notes from Qwen3-8B (4-bit) for 250 test claims, citing only the top-3 snippets; out-of-range citations are removed and notes are cut to 2 sentences.
3. Faithfulness (zero-shot NLI, p_entail > 0.5) is low: 0.32 overall (EN 0.29, AR 0.48), citation accuracy 0.31; 21% of notes are fully faithful.
4. Most unfaithful sentences explain a contradiction by absence ("no mention of X") or meta wording ("the claim is contradicted because…"), which snippet-level NLI cannot entail; this is a limit of the metric as much as of the notes, worth discussing in the report.
